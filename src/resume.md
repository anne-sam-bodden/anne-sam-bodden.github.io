## Profile

I help organizations make better decisions with their data, and I am at my best when the business is changing underneath it. At Cable One I work with Product, Marketing, Finance, and executives on product performance, customer retention, pricing, and revenue, and I build the tools they use to answer those questions themselves, including dashboards, lookup tools, and campaign tracking. When a billing migration, a new system, or a product launch moves the numbers, I follow them until I understand why, and I keep customers and revenue straight along the way.

I came to analytics after two decades in telecom billing and business operations, where I supervised a billing team of ten, carried billing through company mergers, and later ran finance, contracts, and HR for a software consulting firm. With that experience and a degree in accounting, I understand how a business runs as well as how to measure it. I do my best work close to the data and to the people who depend on it, and I am comfortable leading a team when the work calls for it.
